      This is a simplified 2D model for comparing layouts, not a CFD study. Fans are rated at 60 CFM (120mm) and 75 CFM (140mm) at full speed. A radiator cuts a fan's flow by about 22% and an intake dust filter by about 8%. Interior fans only move air that is already in the case. Temperatures are rough rises above a 22 °C room, and a GPU's core temperature tends to move about one-for-one with the temperature of the air it pulls in.
